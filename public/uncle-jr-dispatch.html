I.
📼 The Recap
What Just Happened

Well, well, well — Bills Mafia, pull up a folding chair, pour yourself somethin' cold (and KEEP it under one), 'cause I'm about to take you on a ride. Where do I even begin? Last season was a whirlwind — the good, the bad, and the medusa-ugly all rolled into one big greasy bag of Duff's wings. We came outta the gate lookin' like world-beaters — 4 and 0, the LAST undefeated team standing in the entire NFL, hadn't seen that since 2014 they tell me.

Then Drake Maye and the Patriots strolled into Foxborough Week 5, ran TreVeyon Henderson straight down our throat, and reminded me what I've been preaching for the longest — you can't go 17-0 if your front seven can't fit a gap on third-and-short. We bounced back, sure — even snapped the Pats' 10-game winning streak with that 35-31 comeback in December, biggest road comeback by a visiting team in Gillette Stadium history, you hear me?

But then come Week 12, the Houston Texans looked us dead in the eye, took the football, walked it into the end zone, and stuck us with a head-to-head L that cost us the AFC East tiebreaker for the first time since 2019. 12-5 we finished, 7-2 at home, and if you weren't in Orchard Park for the LAST EVER game at the old Highmark Stadium, my brother, you missed something sacred — they're knockin' that lady down and openin' the new house this fall.

II.
🔧 Currently Going On
The Offseason Chess Match

Now pull up closer, 'cause this is where the chess game gets spicy. The draft? Mixed bag, lemme be real witcha — Big Baller Beane traded back outta the first round THREE times, had me damn near snappin' a 2x4 over my knee in the workshop — but when I saw the haul come in, I had to tip my cap. We turned picks 26, 91, and 165 into 35, 66, 101, 125, and 167 — that's modernized thinkin', the league office their words, not mine.

Round 2 at 35 we snatched T.J. Parker, that Clemson edge who lives in the backfield. Round 2 at 62 we grabbed Davison Igbinosun, an Ohio State corner who plays NASTY — yeah the kid catches flags, but I don't trust no corner who won't punch a receiver in the mouth.

And then in Round 4 at pick 125, we STOLE — and I do mean stole, the consensus board had him at 83 — Skyler Bell out of UConn. 4.4 forty, 41-inch vertical, 11-foot-1 broad jump, 1,278 yards and 13 touchdowns last year, Biletnikoff finalist, Senior Bowl invite, Consensus All-American.

My nephew Dwayne — runs that breakdown channel outta his garage in Cheektowaga — broke down Bell's tape three nights straight and called me at midnight talkin' 'bout: "Unc, the kid's yards-per-route-run was 3.13 last season — that's Puka Nacua territory!" Multiple scouts told my other nephew Jason over at the beat that this kid contributes Day One.

Whether he's that DAWG we need in 11-personnel packages, we'll find out in St. John Fisher come July — but I got a feeling, Mafia, I got a feeling.

Now D.J. Moore — let's talk it. We sent the 60th pick to Chicago to bring him home, and yeah some folks clutched they pearls, but you reunite that man with Joe Brady — yes, OUR Joe Brady, the new head coach who was Carolina's OC when D.J. went over 1,100 yards TWO seasons in a row — and what you got is a real WR1 for the kid. Third-most receiving EPA against man coverage since 2023. Translation, baby: when teams try to single him up, he EATS.

?

Is D.J. Moore the missin' piece that finally cements this offense — or is he just gonna be the next ghost of WRs past?

— The question Bills Mafia better be askin'

And then there's Bradley Chubb — Chubbs to me — three years, $43.5 million, $29 million guaranteed, comin' off 8.5 sacks in Miami. Chubbs is 29, two full years removed from that ACL, and now he's gotta translate to Jim Leonhard's brand new 3-4 defense — yeah, you heard me right, McDermott's 4-3 nickel base is GONE, dead and buried with the old building. We talkin' a complete schematic overhaul.
